# Verelle store listing draft

## Name and subtitle

**Verelle**

Private Marquette Method charting

## Short description

Record and display your daily Marquette Method observations in a calm, private chart.

## Full description

Verelle is a record-and-display chart for people already learning or using the Marquette Method with an instructor.

Record cycle starts, monitor readings, quantitative hormones, LH strips, bleeding, mucus, temperature, intercourse, notes, and your own Peak marker. Review each fact by cycle day with mechanical counts anchored only to events you marked.

Your chart works locally without an account or network connection. Optional end-to-end encrypted sync lets your authorized devices—and one partner you explicitly invite—share the same chart. Verelle’s server stores encrypted chart operations and does not hold the chart key.

Export your chart as JSON or CSV and delete individual observations or the account from the app. No ads, no analytics SDK, and no sale of health data.

Verelle displays what you enter. It does not interpret fertility, predict fertile windows, recommend actions, diagnose conditions, or replace instruction or medical care.

“Marquette Method” is used nominatively to describe compatibility. Verelle is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Marquette University or the Boland Institute for Natural Family Planning. Final nominative-use wording requires the Phase 7 legal review.
