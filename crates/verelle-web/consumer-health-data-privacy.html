Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective and policy version: July 20, 2026

Verelle is operated by Jacob Stephens. This policy describes the consumer health data Verelle collects, why it collects it, where it comes from, the limited parties involved in processing it, and how you can exercise your rights.

Categories of consumer health data collected and purposes

Chart observations
Cycle dates and starts; monitor setup and readings; quantitative E3G and LH values; LH-strip results; bleeding, intercourse, cervical-mucus, temperature, note, and user-marked Peak records that you choose to enter. Verelle uses these facts only to create, display, export, and—if you enable an account—synchronize your chart between authorized devices and a partner you explicitly invite. Chart content is encrypted on your device before transmission. Verelle's server stores ciphertext and does not hold the chart decryption keys.
Account, consent, and device data
Your email address passes through the service over TLS during registration and sign-in and is transformed into a SHA-256 credential identifier; the server does not retain the email address itself. Because email addresses can be guessed, this identifier is still treated as sensitive account data. The service retains that identifier, OPAQUE password-registration data, account/device/dataset identifiers, your device public key, encrypted key wrappers, session-token hashes, and the version and time of your affirmative consent. These data are used for registration, authentication, account security, encrypted-key custody, and documenting consent.
Service and synchronization metadata
IP address, browser user-agent, request time and path, operation identifiers, encrypted payload size, synchronization sequence and timing, key epoch, deletion identifiers and timestamps, and security/availability events. Verelle uses these data to deliver requests, synchronize encrypted operations, prevent abuse, investigate failures or security incidents, operate backups, and honor deletion requests. Verelle does not derive fertility status or create advertising profiles from this metadata.
Subscription data
If you purchase optional encrypted synchronization, Stripe collects your billing contact and payment information under its privacy terms. Verelle receives and stores the Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, entitlement status, billing period, grace and retention dates, and signed webhook event metadata. Verelle does not receive or store full payment-card details. Local charting and export do not require a subscription.

Sources

Chart observations and the email address come directly from you. Account keys, recovery material, device identifiers, public keys, and encrypted operations are created by your browser or app. Network, security, and synchronization metadata are created automatically when your device communicates with the service. Verelle does not obtain consumer health data from data brokers, advertisers, social networks, or unrelated third parties.

Sharing, processors, and sale

Verelle does not sell consumer health data. It does not share consumer health data with third parties or affiliates for their own purposes, advertising, or profiling. Verelle has no affiliates.

Verelle uses these service providers as processors solely to operate the product you request:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. may transiently proxy HTTPS traffic and receive network-error reports while DNS changes propagate or if edge proxying is enabled. In that role it processes IP address, request and response traffic (including the email address during registration), page/request paths, browser and connection metadata, OPAQUE messages, and already-encrypted chart ciphertext.
  • DigitalOcean, LLC hosts the web service and PostgreSQL database in the United States. It processes encrypted chart ciphertext, account and sync metadata, and network logs.
  • Amazon Web Services, Inc. provides S3 storage in the United States for encrypted-at-rest database backups. Backups contain chart ciphertext and the account and service metadata present in PostgreSQL.
  • Stripe, Inc. hosts optional web checkout, processes payment and billing-contact information, and returns customer, subscription, invoice, refund, and entitlement events. Stripe does not receive chart observations from Verelle.
  • Apple Inc. and Google LLC distribute the mobile and watch apps and may process store-account, device, crash, and push-delivery metadata under their policies. Push messages, when enabled, contain only an opaque instruction to synchronize and no chart observation.

If you create and privately send a one-use couple invitation, the person who proves possession of that invitation can join your encrypted dataset and receive chart history and future entries. The invite secret and chart key are inside the code and never available to Verelle. Do not send an invitation to anyone you do not intend to authorize. Unlinking stops future access after key rotation but cannot erase copies already downloaded to another person’s device.

Your rights and how to exercise them

You may ask Verelle to confirm whether it is collecting, sharing, or selling your consumer health data; access that data and the list of recipients; withdraw consent from future collection; or delete your consumer health data. You may exercise these rights in either of these ways:

  • While signed in, use Delete account and chart in the Verelle app for authenticated deletion of the account and encrypted server chart.
  • Email jacob@stephens.page with the subject “Verelle consumer health data request” for access, withdrawal, deletion assistance, or a recipient list.

Verelle will authenticate a request using your existing account and enrolled device or, when reasonably necessary, ask for additional information. It will respond without undue delay and within 45 days, subject to one legally permitted 45-day extension with notice. Withdrawal of collection consent means Verelle can no longer provide account synchronization and will require closure of the hosted account. Deleting one observation commits a deletion marker, redacts known superseded server ciphertext, and removes it from future exports; an offline authorized device may retain an older local copy until it reconnects. Live-system account deletion removes a sole-owner account and encrypted dataset immediately. A shared account must first follow the partner unlink/shared-subject process so one person does not silently erase the other person’s account. Archived or backup copies are removed through backup expiration and, when restoration is necessary, independent deletion-manifest reapplication; completion will not exceed six months from authentication of the request.

Appeals

If Verelle refuses a consumer-rights request, reply to the decision or email jacob@stephens.page with the subject “Appeal of Verelle privacy request.” Verelle will provide a written decision within 45 days. A denied appeal will include a way to contact the appropriate attorney general.

Changes to collection or use

Verelle will update this policy and obtain new affirmative consent before collecting, using, or sharing additional categories of consumer health data, or using existing consumer health data for additional purposes.
